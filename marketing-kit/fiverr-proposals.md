# Fiverr Proposals / Buyer-Request Templates

Reply to a Buyer Request or a direct inquiry with one of these.
Replace every `[placeholder]` before sending. Keep the reply
short — buyers read the first 3 lines and decide. Never promise
a deadline, a result or a feature you cannot deliver. Never
claim Eduvelto is client work — it is the owner's own platform.
