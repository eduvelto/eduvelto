## Long (detailed — website gig, portfolio pages)

Eduvelto (eduvelto.com) is my own project: a free, bilingual
(Bangla + English) education platform for students in
Bangladesh, built with React, Vite and Firebase. It includes a
digital library of 609 books across 2,307 chapters with 23,259
questions (Class 9 to Honours, including Madrasa and Technical
streams); job preparation covering 15 exams (BCS, Bank, teacher
recruitment and more) with verified exam structures; admission
preparation for university, medical, engineering and National
University; a daily 5-question free quiz drawn from 35 verified
BCS English questions; and everyday tools such as a budget
planner, calculators and a CV builder. In total the site holds
1,014 exams across its job, admission and academic sections. The site is fully
bilingual with a persistent language choice, and it is free for
every student.
