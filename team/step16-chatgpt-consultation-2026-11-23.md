## Substance (condensed)

- Product principle: a small, structured Bangla-first career library, NOT
  another examination feature. Teach transferable skills, not supposedly
  fixed examination rules — procedures, eligibility and arrangements can
  differ between circulars and cycles. Fills the Steps 1–15 gap: presenting
  oneself, practical interview/viva preparation, informed career decisions,
  professional CV, connecting preparation to real employment.
- Content: 15 reading items — 6 Viva, 5 Career, 4 CV.
