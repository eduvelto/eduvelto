### 1.4 SPA limit — recorded honestly, not worked around

The served HTML is identical for every route; meta is injected client-side by React.
**Prerendering / SSR is NOT built** — it is an architecture decision needing the owner's
separate approval. Consequence: Google renders the JS, but indexing is slower. This task
does not claim otherwise and does not work around it.

### 1.5 Honesty rules applied (binding)

- No `যাচাইকৃত / verified` unless verified: BCS + Primary Teacher are `verified`;
  NTRCA is `last_verified` and is labelled `শেষ` (last verified pattern, 18th exam).
  Bank's subject split is verified but its negative marking is NOT verified and is
  labelled so. The other 10 job exams are `not_verified` (IT-professional: scope pending)
  and say plainly that the pattern is unverified.
- **No claims about mock tests** (no published mock is live). Practice/mocks are
  described everywhere as `শীঘ্রই আসছে` (coming soon).
- No claims about AI Teacher, smart planner, or unbuilt features. `/ai-teacher` and
  `/skills` pages themselves say `শীঘ্রই আসছে`.
- No `#1` as fact. The framing is `সেরা ফ্রি` (best free) only — an ambition backed by
  the real free offer — never `১ নম্বর` as a fact. A unit test enforces this
  (`src/lib/__tests__/seo.test.ts`, 28 tests; results in §3.1).
- No invented numbers, reviews, or popularity claims anywhere in the copy.

### 1.6 Login-gating — verified against the sitemap

The sitemap was checked for chapter/lesson content URLs: **none are listed**. Grep for
`books/`, `lessons/`, `quiz`, `chapter` in `public/sitemap.xml` returns **0 matches**.
This matches the owner's decisions in §6.5: hub/listing pages are public + crawlable;
all content behind them (chapter/lesson reading, questions, practice, mock, results,
progress) is login-gated and excluded from the sitemap.
