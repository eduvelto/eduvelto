## 1. What Eduvelto is

Eduvelto is a free education platform for Bangladesh: a digital library,
lessons, free exams, progress tracking and general health information.
Core learning is free. No payments are taken inside Eduvelto, and there
are no paid subscriptions at launch.

## 2. Accounts

- You must give true information when you register. You are responsible
  for keeping your password secret and for everything done with your
  account.
- One account per person. Do not share your account or let anyone else
  use it.
- Students under 18 should register with a guardian's knowledge.
  Guardian accounts link to a child only after a free manual
  verification (written information, the child's confirmation, and
  Admin activation). No identity verification is required for student
  accounts, and no verification fee is charged to anyone.
- We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, after a
  warning where appropriate. The Owner account cannot be removed or
  downgraded by any other administrator.

## 3. Acceptable use

You must NOT:
- Cheat in exams, or help others cheat, or copy answers from outside the
  exam;
- Upload or share copyrighted material you do not own or have permission
  to share. Note: the app has NO upload feature today — you cannot
  upload videos, files or images. If uploading is enabled in future,
  it will be limited to admin-approved creators, verified teachers and
  verified institutions, and the same copyright rules will apply;
- Harass, threaten, bully or harm anyone, especially children;
- Share anyone's private information (phone, email, address, photos)
  without their permission;
- Try to break, overload or misuse the platform, or access data that is
  not yours;
- Pretend to be someone else, including a teacher, guardian or Admin.

Breaking these rules can lead to a warning, suspension, or a permanent
ban, and to a report to the authorities where the law requires it.

## 4. Content

- Eduvelto's lessons, questions and practice exams are original practice
  material written for Eduvelto. They are NOT board or university exam
  questions, and they are labelled as practice questions.
- Textbook content is paraphrased and summarised, never reproduced
  verbatim. Official sources are cited where available.
- You keep ownership of anything you write in the app (for example, your
  exam answers). By submitting it, you allow Eduvelto to store and show
  it back to you and to the people allowed to see it (your teacher, your
  guardian — progress and results only).
- Do not assume that editing a copyrighted video or text makes it safe
  to publish. Copyright is respected and reported content is removed.

## 5. Learning results and health information

- Exam results are practice and self-test results, not official board or
  university results. Eduvelto does not guarantee any exam outcome,
  admission, grade or job.
- Health information on Eduvelto is general information only. It is not
  a diagnosis, not medical advice, and does not replace a doctor.
  Every health page says this, and health content is reviewed before it
  is published. If you feel unwell, see a doctor; in an emergency call
  999.
- We work to keep information correct, but we cannot promise it is
  always complete or error-free. Check important information against an
  official source.

## 6. Advertising

General, non-personalised, child-safe advertising may appear in the app
in future (AdSense/AdMob). No video ads, no creator monetisation and no
paid content exist at launch. Ads never cover lessons, quizzes, exam
controls or essential navigation.

## 7. Availability and changes

Eduvelto is provided "as is". We work to keep it available, but we do
not promise it will never be down or free of errors. Features may be
added, changed or removed. If these terms change, users are told in-app
before the change takes effect.

## 8. Privacy

How we collect, use and protect your data is in the Privacy Policy,
